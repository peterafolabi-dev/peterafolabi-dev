012026 · Fintech · Own product

Oja Ledger

Offline double-entry bookkeeping for Nigerian traders. Record a sale in two taps with no network, then get profit, VAT returns and the small-company tax check under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025.

The hard part

Every entry is sealed with a SHA-256 hash of the one before it, so editing any past naira is detectable. Money is whole kobo, never floats, and 800 random trading histories are tested on every push.

02Aug – Oct 2026 · Marketplace · MALhub internship

Travel & Stays

A two-sided marketplace for verified stays and local tours across Nigeria, with guests, hosts and tour guides, Paystack payments in naira, and an admin dashboard.

The hard part

Two guests can't book the same dates, even in the same second: a Postgres exclude using gist constraint makes the double booking impossible. A booking is only marked paid after the server confirms the exact amount and currency with Paystack, covered by 11 unit tests.

03Dec 2025 – now · E-commerce · Own product

RedCart

An online store with 103 products, Google sign-in, Paystack checkout, review fraud scoring, an AI shopping assistant, and a second storefront in Next.js reading the store's API.

The hard part

The AI assistant isn't allowed to guess. Order status and stock come from the database, and policy answers come from real documents through a small retrieval layer. Payments are re-checked against the cart total on the server before an order exists. 94 tests run in CI.
